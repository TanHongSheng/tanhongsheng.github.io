Game Rule

  1. The Golden Number is 21.
  2. Players take turns counting upward, starting from 1.
  3. On each turn, a player may say either 1 number or 2 consecutive numbers.
  4. The player who says 21 wins the game.

Strategy Hint

Try to end your turns on:

3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18

These numbers help you control the path to 21.
