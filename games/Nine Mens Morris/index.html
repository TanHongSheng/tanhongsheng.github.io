    Complete rules
    • Each player has nine pieces. White always moves first.
    • Place one piece per turn. A mill is three pieces in a connected horizontal, vertical, or diagonal marked line.
    • After making a mill, remove one opposing piece. Normally, a piece in a mill is protected unless every opposing piece is in a mill.
    • After all pieces are placed, move along the marked lines to an adjacent empty point. Pieces cannot jump.
    • A player with four or fewer pieces may fly one piece to any empty point. If that player forms a mill, they may remove any opposing piece, including one protected inside a mill.
    • You win when your opponent has fewer than three pieces or no legal move.
    • If both players have three pieces and nobody wins within 10 complete rounds, the game is a draw. One round gives each player one turn.
