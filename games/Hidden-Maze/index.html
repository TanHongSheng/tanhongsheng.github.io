Hidden Maze Race
Local pass-and-play

Game rules

Build. Hide. Race.

  • 1. Build: Each player secretly builds a 4 × 4 maze for the other player. Add up to 8 inside walls and keep at least one route from START to FINISH.
  • 2. Hide: Pass the device only when the privacy screen appears. The maze walls stay invisible to the player solving them.
  • 3. Turns: Player A takes a turn, then Player B, and the order repeats. Take up to 3 steps using the arrow buttons, arrow keys, or W/A/S/D. After the third safe step, the game hides the board and opens the pass screen automatically.
  • 4. Crash: If you hit any wall—including the outside border—you return to START. The exact wall is revealed once. Press Continue to hide it and end your turn.
  • 5. Remember: The one-time reveal disappears after Continue and is not recorded. Use your memory.
  • 6. Color: Each player chooses a different interface color, making it clear whose private screen is showing.
  • 7. Win: Both players finish the round. If both reach FINISH in that round, the player who used fewer steps in the final turn wins; the same step is a tie.
