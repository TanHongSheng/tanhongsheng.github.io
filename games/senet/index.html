How to Play

  1. Each player has 4 pieces. Follow squares 1→24 and bear off all four pieces first.
  2. On your turn, roll two fair five-faced dice (2D5). Choose one result to use.
  3. If only one rolled value gives a legal move, you must use it. If neither gives a legal move, your turn ends.
  4. You get a bonus roll after moving if the chosen value is 1, the chosen value is 5, or you capture an opponent piece. A 4 gives no bonus.
  5. Land on an unprotected opponent piece to swap places. Two adjacent friendly pieces protect each other.
  6. You must move forward if possible. If there is no legal forward move, move backward by the chosen amount if possible.
  7. 20 · House of Beauty: every piece must land exactly on 20 before it may pass beyond it.
  8. 21 · House of Water: landing here sends the piece back to square 12, or the nearest free square before 12.
  9. Final Houses are locked: a piece on 22 leaves only with 3; on 23 only with 2; on 24 only with 1.
  10. The first player to bear off all four pieces wins.

Game Log

Senet's original rulebook did not survive. This compact custom version uses 24 squares, four pieces each, 2D5 choice, adjacency protection, bonus rolls on 1, 5, or capture, and exact 3/2/1 exits from squares 22/23/24.
