Game Rules

  1. A full match has two halves. In the first half, Player A is the Attacker and Player B is the Defender. In the second half, they switch roles.
  2. At the start of each half, both players have seven cards: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. Each card can be used only once in that half.
  3. Each half has seven rounds. In each round, the Attacker secretly chooses one unused card and chooses a public bet from 1 to 7 points.
  4. The Defender sees only the bet amount. Then the Defender secretly chooses one unused card.
  5. As soon as the Defender locks the card, both cards are revealed and compared. The larger card wins the round. Equal cards are a tie.
  6. If the Attacker wins, the Attacker gains the bet amount. If the Defender wins, the Attacker loses the bet amount. If the round is tied, the Attacker gains 0.
  7. After both players have taken one half as Attacker, compare the two final scores. The higher score wins the match.

Classroom note: use points or tokens only. This version is designed as a probability and strategy game, not real-money play.
