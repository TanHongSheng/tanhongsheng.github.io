How the online version works

GitHub Pages shows the game page. Firebase stores a tiny room state, such as the round number, used cards, public bet, and reveal result.

The chosen cards are not written directly at first. The page stores a hidden commitment first, then reveals the card after both players lock in.

Game Rules

  1. Two players play two attack turns. Player A attacks first; Player B attacks second.
  2. Each player has cards 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. A used card cannot be used again during the current attack turn.
  3. In each round, the Attacker secretly chooses one unused card and announces a bet amount from 1 to 7 points.
  4. The Defender sees the bet amount, then secretly chooses one unused card.
  5. Both cards are revealed. Larger card wins the round.
  6. If the Attacker wins, the Attacker gains the bet amount. If the Attacker loses, the Attacker loses the bet amount. If tied, the Attacker gains 0.
  7. After 7 rounds, the players switch roles and reset their cards. After the second attack turn, the higher score wins.

Classroom note: use points or tokens only. This version is designed as a probability and strategy game, not real-money play.
