Exploding Hands

A two-player adding-and-splitting game. Attack your opponent’s hands, repair your own hands before Sudden Death, and try to make both opponent hands explode.

Game Rules

  1. Each player starts with two hands: 1/1.
  2. On your turn, either attack or redistribute.
  3. To attack, use one alive hand to add its power to one opponent hand.
  4. If a hand reaches 5 or more, it explodes and becomes 0.
  5. A 0-hand cannot attack.
  6. To redistribute, keep the same total power, but split it differently between your two hands.
  7. Redistribution must leave both hands alive, so each hand must be between 1 and 4.
  8. After both players have taken 8 turns, Sudden Death begins. From then on, only attacks are allowed.
  9. The first player whose two hands are both 0/0 loses.

Strategy Hint

  • Try to explode one opponent hand first.
  • Before Sudden Death, avoid having only one alive hand.
  • Good Sudden Death targets are 2/3, 3/3, 2/4, and 3/4.
